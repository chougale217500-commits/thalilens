Ordering well when eating out

Restaurant food is usually heavier than home food because of extra butter, cream and oil. You do not have to avoid eating out. Choosing a tandoori or dal dish over a cream gravy, and roti over butter naan, often saves more than 300 kcal in one meal.

After the food arrives, you can analyse a photo of your plate for the actual portion.

Questions

What kind of photo works?

A clear, straight photo of one page of the menu with readable dish names. If the menu is long, take one photo per page.

How does it choose the better picks?

It looks at how each dish is usually cooked: grilled, steamed and dal or vegetable dishes rank above deep-fried and cream-based ones. The calories are rough values for a typical restaurant serving.

Does it know this restaurant's recipes?

No. It only reads the dish names. The same dish can be much heavier in one restaurant than another.
