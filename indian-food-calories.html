How to read this chart

A katori here means a small steel bowl of about 150 ml. Weights are for cooked food. If your katori is bigger or you take a second helping, multiply the numbers. One teaspoon of ghee or oil adds about 45 kcal, and one teaspoon of sugar adds about 20 kcal, so small additions change the total quickly.

Why the same dish can have different calories

Dal made with one teaspoon of ghee and dal finished with butter and cream are both called dal, but the second can have twice the fat. The same is true for paratha, sabzi and biryani. Recipes change from region to region and from house to house, so treat every number as a starting point.

Build a balanced thali

  • Fill half the plate with sabzi and salad for fibre.
  • Add one protein item at every meal: dal, rajma, chole, curd, paneer, egg, fish or chicken.
  • Keep roti and rice together to about a quarter of the plate.
  • Keep fried snacks and sweets as occasional items.
