Reading a packet in ten seconds

The front of a packet is advertising. The back is information. Three numbers per 100 g tell most of the story: sugar, salt and saturated fat. This tool pulls them out and marks each as low, medium or high.

Product data: Open Food Facts, available under the Open Database Licence.

Questions

Where does the product information come from?

From Open Food Facts, a free database of packaged foods built by volunteers around the world. Not every Indian product is in it yet.

What if my product is not found?

Take a photo of the nutrition table on the pack and use the photo analyser on the home page. It reads the label for you.

What do low, medium and high mean?

They compare sugar, fat, saturated fat and salt per 100 g with widely used front-of-pack label thresholds for foods. Drinks use lower thresholds, so read drinks with care.
