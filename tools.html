Free nutrition tools for Indian food

Every tool is free and runs in your browser. Nothing to install.

  • Food photo analyser

    Take a photo, type or say what you ate. Get the dish, ingredients, full nutrition, a balance score and lighter swaps in 10 languages.

  • Recipe nutrition calculator

    Paste the ingredients of a home recipe and get calories and macros per serving.

  • Restaurant menu scanner

    Photograph a menu and see which dishes are the better picks and which are the heaviest.

  • Barcode scanner for packaged food

    Scan or type the barcode on a packet to see sugar, salt, fat and how processed it is.

  • Compare two foods

    Put two Indian foods side by side: roti or rice, idli or dosa, samosa or vada pav.

  • Daily calorie and BMI calculator

    Estimate how many calories and how much protein you need in a day.

  • Calories burned calculator

    See what walking, running, yoga and 12 other activities use, in terms of everyday food.

  • Indian food calorie chart

    Calories and macros for 28 common foods, with a page for each.
