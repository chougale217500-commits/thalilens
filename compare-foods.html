Questions

Is roti or rice better?

Two phulka have about 150 kcal and one katori of rice about 195 kcal, so they are close. Roti has a little more fibre and protein. The portion matters more than the choice.

Are the values for the same weight?

No. Each food is shown for its usual serving, such as one roti or one katori, because that is how people eat. Each food page also gives values per 100 g.
