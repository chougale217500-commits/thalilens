Why calculate a recipe

A photo shows what is on the plate. A recipe shows what went into the pot. If you cook the same dal, sabzi or kheer every week, working it out once from the ingredients gives you a number you can reuse, and it shows exactly how much the ghee, oil and sugar add.

To check a cooked plate, use the photo analyser. For quick numbers, see the calorie chart.

Questions

How do I enter a recipe?

Write one ingredient per line with its amount, for example "1 cup toor dal" or "2 tsp ghee". Then enter how many servings the recipe makes.

Are the results exact?

No. They are estimates based on typical values for each ingredient. Brands, the size of your cup and how much oil is absorbed all change the result.

Can I use Hindi names for ingredients?

Yes. Names such as atta, besan, jeera, haldi and paneer work, and you can write in Hindi or English.
