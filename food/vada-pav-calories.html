Vada pav calories and nutrition

वड़ा पाव · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of vada pav (1 vada pav, about 140 g) has about 300 kcal, with 7 g protein, 42 g carbohydrate and 12 g fat. That is about 15% of a 2,000 kcal day.

What is vada pav?

A fried potato vada in a pav with dry garlic chutney, the classic Mumbai street snack.

How to make it lighter

Skip the butter and the fried chilli, and have one with a glass of chaas instead of a second vada pav.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Occasionally. It is fried and low in protein and fibre.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 300 kcal in roughly 65 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
