Paneer butter masala calories and nutrition

पनीर बटर मसाला · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of paneer butter masala (1 katori, 150 g) has about 320 kcal, with 12 g protein, 12 g carbohydrate and 25 g fat. That is about 16% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Fat from paneer, butter and cream. About 70 percent of the calories are fat.

What is paneer butter masala?

Paneer cubes in a tomato gravy enriched with butter, cream and often cashew paste.

How to make it lighter

At home, skip the cream, use milk and a few cashews, and do not fry the paneer.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Occasionally. For regular meals, palak paneer or paneer bhurji gives the protein with less fat.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 320 kcal in roughly 70 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
