Plain dosa calories and nutrition

सादा डोसा · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of plain dosa (1 dosa, 90 g) has about 170 kcal, with 4 g protein, 28 g carbohydrate and 5 g fat. That is about 8% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Batter and the oil on the tawa. Restaurant dosas are larger and use more oil or butter.

What is plain dosa?

A thin crisp crepe of fermented rice and urad dal batter cooked on a tawa.

How to make it lighter

Ask for less oil, and choose plain over ghee roast or butter dosa.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes. One or two plain dosas with sambar make a reasonable meal.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 170 kcal in roughly 35 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.

Questions

How many calories are in plain dosa?

One serving of plain dosa (1 dosa, 90 g) has about 170 kcal. Per 100 g that is about 188.9 kcal. The value changes with the recipe and the portion.

How much protein is in plain dosa?

One serving of plain dosa (1 dosa, 90 g) gives about 4 g of protein, along with 28 g of carbohydrate and 5 g of fat.

Is plain dosa good for weight loss?

Yes. One or two plain dosas with sambar make a reasonable meal. Weight change depends on your total food and activity over the day, not on one dish.
