Jeera rice calories and nutrition

जीरा राइस · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of jeera rice (1 katori, 150 g) has about 210 kcal, with 4 g protein, 40 g carbohydrate and 4 g fat. That is about 10% of a 2,000 kcal day.

The same starch as plain rice, plus one to two teaspoons of fat per serving in restaurant versions.

What is jeera rice?

Basmati rice tempered with cumin seeds in oil or ghee.

How to make it lighter

At home, use one teaspoon of ghee for the whole pot. The cumin flavour does not need more.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes, like plain rice, as long as the portion is one katori.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 210 kcal in roughly 45 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.

Questions

How many calories are in jeera rice?

One serving of jeera rice (1 katori, 150 g) has about 210 kcal. Per 100 g that is about 140 kcal. The value changes with the recipe and the portion.

How much protein is in jeera rice?

One serving of jeera rice (1 katori, 150 g) gives about 4 g of protein, along with 40 g of carbohydrate and 4 g of fat.

Is jeera rice good for weight loss?

Yes, like plain rice, as long as the portion is one katori. Weight change depends on your total food and activity over the day, not on one dish.
