Upma calories and nutrition

उपमा · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of upma (1 katori, 150 g) has about 220 kcal, with 5 g protein, 33 g carbohydrate and 8 g fat. That is about 11% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Carbohydrate 59%Protein 9%Fat 32%

Semolina and the oil or ghee used for roasting and tempering.

What is upma?

Roasted semolina cooked with water, vegetables and a tempering of mustard and curry leaves.

How to make it lighter

Cook it with plenty of vegetables and use one teaspoon of oil per serving.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes, in a katori portion. It is low in protein, so add curd or milk.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 220 kcal in roughly 45 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
