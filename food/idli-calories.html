Idli calories and nutrition

इडली · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of idli (2 pieces, 80 g) has about 120 kcal, with 4 g protein, 24 g carbohydrate and 0.5 g fat. That is about 6% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Carbohydrate 82%Protein 14%Fat 4%

Rice starch. There is almost no fat because idli is steamed.

What is idli?

Steamed cakes of fermented rice and urad dal batter.

How to make it lighter

Take a full katori of sambar for protein and go easy on coconut chutney.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes. It is one of the lightest Indian breakfasts. Add sambar so it keeps you full.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 120 kcal in roughly 25 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.

Questions

How many calories are in idli?

One serving of idli (2 pieces, 80 g) has about 120 kcal. Per 100 g that is about 150 kcal. The value changes with the recipe and the portion.

How much protein is in idli?

One serving of idli (2 pieces, 80 g) gives about 4 g of protein, along with 24 g of carbohydrate and 0.5 g of fat.

Is idli good for weight loss?

Yes. It is one of the lightest Indian breakfasts. Add sambar so it keeps you full. Weight change depends on your total food and activity over the day, not on one dish.
