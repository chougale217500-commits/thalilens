Sambar calories and nutrition

सांभर · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of sambar (1 katori, 150 g) has about 110 kcal, with 5 g protein, 16 g carbohydrate and 3 g fat. That is about 6% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Dal and vegetables. It is low in fat unless the tempering is heavy.

What is sambar?

A thin stew of toor dal, vegetables and tamarind with sambar masala.

How to make it lighter

Add more vegetables such as drumstick, pumpkin and brinjal.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes. It adds protein and fibre to idli and dosa for few calories.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 110 kcal in roughly 25 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
