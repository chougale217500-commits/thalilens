Poha calories and nutrition

पोहा · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of poha (1 plate, 150 g) has about 250 kcal, with 5 g protein, 42 g carbohydrate and 7 g fat. That is about 12% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Carbohydrate 67%Protein 8%Fat 25%

Rice flakes, oil in the tempering, and toppings such as sev and extra peanuts.

What is poha?

Flattened rice cooked with onion, mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric and peanuts.

How to make it lighter

Add peas or other vegetables, measure the oil, and replace sev with lemon and coriander.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

Yes, with protein added. A bowl of curd or a boiled egg on the side makes it more filling.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 250 kcal in roughly 55 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.

Questions

How many calories are in poha?

One serving of poha (1 plate, 150 g) has about 250 kcal. Per 100 g that is about 166.7 kcal. The value changes with the recipe and the portion.

How much protein is in poha?

One serving of poha (1 plate, 150 g) gives about 5 g of protein, along with 42 g of carbohydrate and 7 g of fat.

Is poha good for weight loss?

Yes, with protein added. A bowl of curd or a boiled egg on the side makes it more filling. Weight change depends on your total food and activity over the day, not on one dish.
