Samosa calories and nutrition

समोसा · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of samosa (1 samosa, 80 g) has about 250 kcal, with 4 g protein, 28 g carbohydrate and 14 g fat. That is about 12% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Where the calories come from

Carbohydrate 44%Protein 6%Fat 50%

Frying oil. About half the calories are fat absorbed by the crust.

What is samosa?

A deep-fried maida pastry filled with spiced potato and peas.

How to make it lighter

Share one, or choose a baked or air-fried samosa, and have it with unsweetened chai.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

As a treat, not a habit. Two samosas equal a full meal in calories with little protein.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 250 kcal in roughly 55 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
