Aloo paratha calories and nutrition

आलू पराठा · Vegetarian · Updated 13 November 2026

One serving of aloo paratha (1 paratha, 120 g) has about 290 kcal, with 6 g protein, 40 g carbohydrate and 12 g fat. That is about 14% of a 2,000 kcal day.

Dough, potato filling and ghee all add up, and the white butter served on top can add another 50 to 100 kcal.

What is aloo paratha?

Aloo paratha is a wheat paratha stuffed with spiced mashed potato, usually eaten with curd, butter or pickle.

How to make it lighter

Roast with little ghee, eat it with plain curd instead of butter, and keep it to one paratha with a bowl of curd.

Does it fit a weight-loss diet?

As an occasional breakfast, yes. Two parathas with butter can cross 700 kcal, so the portion matters.

How long to burn it off

A person of 65 kg uses about 290 kcal in roughly 60 minutes of brisk walking. Try your own weight and activity in the calories burned calculator.
