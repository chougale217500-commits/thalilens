Calories in Chai, Coffee, Lassi and Other Indian Drinks

Updated 11 November 2026 · By the Thali Lens team

Drinks are the calories people forget to count. They do not fill the stomach the way food does, and many of us have them four or five times a day.

Sugar by the teaspoon

One teaspoon of sugar is about 20 kcal. Four cups of chai with two teaspoons each means eight teaspoons, or 160 kcal from sugar alone. Cutting to one teaspoon per cup halves that without giving up tea.

Milk matters too

The 50 kcal in an unsweetened cup come from milk. Chai made with more milk than water, or with full-cream milk, is higher. Milk also brings protein and calcium, so it is the sugar, not the milk, that is worth reducing first.

Lassi and chaas

Both start from curd. Sweet lassi adds sugar and often malai, which takes a glass to about 240 kcal. Chaas is curd thinned with water and salt, about 50 kcal a glass, and works well with lunch.

Juice and soft drinks

Sugarcane juice, packaged juice and cola are mostly sugar in water. Whole fruit gives the same sweetness with fibre and is more filling.

Quick tips

  • Reduce sugar in tea slowly, half a teaspoon at a time. Taste adjusts in a couple of weeks.
  • Drink water first when you feel thirsty.
  • Choose chaas, plain nimbu pani or coconut water over soft drinks.
