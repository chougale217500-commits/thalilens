Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information Thali Lens ("we", "the site") handles when you use this website.

Photos you analyse

When you press Analyse, your image is resized in your browser and sent to our server, which passes it to our AI service provider to produce the nutrition estimate. The provider may use submitted content to improve its services. We do not save your photo or the result on our servers after the response is returned. Do not upload photos that show people, documents or other personal information.

Accounts and sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. We receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use this only to identify your account and to prevent misuse of the service. We do not sell it. You can delete your account at any time from the Account page, which removes this information from our sign-in records.

Voice, barcode and saved history

If you use the Speak button, your browser's speech service turns your voice into text; we receive only the text. Barcode lookups are sent from your browser to Open Food Facts, a public food database. Your daily goal, today's log and the 7-day history are stored only in your own browser and are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. Questions you ask about a meal, recipes you paste and menu photos are sent to our AI service provider in the same way as food photos, and are not saved by us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit. We use this to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

We may use an analytics service to understand how many people use the site and which pages are useful. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask a question or make a request about your data, write to contact@YOURDOMAIN.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Thali Lens estimates nutrition for Indian food from a photo. Estimates are not medical advice.
